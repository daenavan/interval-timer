Interval Timer

A free, simple interval timer for HIIT, Tabata, boxing rounds and circuit training. The whole screen turns green for work, red for rest and amber while you get ready, so you can read it from across the room. It beeps at every switch, with optional 3-2-1 warning beeps.

How to use it

Tap the preset name at the top to pick a workout or make your own: work seconds, rest seconds and rounds. Press Start. Tap a block in the timeline to jump to that round. Use ⚙ to change the get-ready countdown, the warning beeps and the timeline.

Install it on your phone

iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open the browser menu and tap Install app. Once installed it works offline, and the screen stays on while the timer runs. On iPhone the beeps play over your music and follow the ring/silent switch.

Your presets and settings are saved only on this device. No account, no tracking.
